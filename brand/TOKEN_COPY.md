# $GAPG listing copy

**Name:** Gapguard · **Ticker:** GAPG · **Logo:** `gapg-logo-1024.png` (also 512 / 256 px and `gapg-logo.svg`)

## Short description (≤ 120 chars)

Gapguard is parametric cover for tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. $GAPG stakers secure its trigger disputes.

## Description (≤ 280 chars)

Gapguard sells on-chain cover for tokenized stocks and RWAs against weekend gaps, depegs, oracle outages and issuer halts, paid automatically when the trigger fires. $GAPG stakers act as the dispute layer for attested triggers and receive a share of cover premiums.

## Notes for the listing

* $GAPG is launched separately; the Gapguard protocol works without it. Token features switch on only after the
  token address is wired through the protocol's 48-hour Timelock (see TOKEN_INTEGRATION.md).
* Staking bonds are slashable when a dispute is wrong. Avoid describing the token as an investment or promising
  returns; premium share depends on cover sales and is not guaranteed.
* Do not use third-party names or logos (issuers, brokers, the chain operator) in token branding.
